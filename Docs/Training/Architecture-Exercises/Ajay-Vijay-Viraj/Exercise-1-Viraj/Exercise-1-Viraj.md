A customer walks in a cafe and wants to buy a  coffee. The barista takes the order and uses the POS (Point Of Sale) Application  a system composed of an order entry component and a payment module  to ring up the purchase. The POS Application processes the transaction by calling the Payment Processing Service, which handles the card payment via the Card Reader device. Once the card is read, the payment terminal records the transaction and confirms. The barista hands over the coffee. The entire flow is tracked as a Transaction Record in the system.
