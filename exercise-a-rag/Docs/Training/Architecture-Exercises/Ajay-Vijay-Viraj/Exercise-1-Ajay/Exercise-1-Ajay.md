The Layered Enterprise Payment System models how a modern café processes customer orders and contactless digital payments using integrated business, application, and technology services. The system begins when a customer places an order at the café. A cashier uses the POS Application to process the order and initiate a contactless payment transaction. The payment request is validated through the Payment Orchestration System and the Banking Integration API, which communicates with the external banking server for payment authorization.

The business layer represents the operational activities of the café, including order processing, payment handling, receipt generation, and inventory updates. Business actors such as the customer, cashier, café manager, and external payment provider interact with business services that support efficient café operations. Business objects such as coffee orders, payment transactions, receipts, and inventory records are created and updated during these processes.

The application layer provides the software capabilities required to support these business activities. Components such as the POS Application, Inventory Management System, and Receipt Service Application realize application services including payment validation, transaction logging, inventory synchronization, and reporting. These services manage data objects such as transaction data and receipt data throughout the payment lifecycle.

The technology layer provides the infrastructure needed to run the applications securely and reliably. Devices such as the POS terminal and NFC card reader connect through the café network router to cloud payment infrastructure and external banking systems. Technology services such as secure communication, cloud hosting, payment gateway services, and monitoring services ensure secure digital transactions and reliable system performance.

The motivation layer explains the business goals and drivers behind the architecture. Goals such as reducing transaction time, improving customer satisfaction, and ensuring reliable payments influence the design of the business, application, and technology layers. Requirements such as secure transaction logging and real-time payment authorization are implemented through the supporting application and infrastructure services.
